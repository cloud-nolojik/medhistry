How It Works
Three steps. Thirty seconds. Done.
No app training needed. No complex setup. Patients upload records once and share them with any doctor, anywhere — using a temporary code.
1

Patient uploads records

Prescriptions, lab reports, discharge summaries — scan paper or upload digital. AI reads and organizes everything into a structured health profile.

2

Patient shares a temporary code

At the clinic, a rotating 6-digit code appears in the patient's app — like Google Authenticator. The patient reviews what will be shared and gives consent.

3

Doctor enters code, gets briefing

Doctor types the 6 digits in their app. Instantly sees a structured briefing card — conditions, meds, allergies, labs. Session auto-expires after the visit.

Security & Trust
Built for privacy, designed for India
Patient data never lingers. Doctor access is temporary. Everything is consent-based and compliant with Indian data protection law.
🔒

Consent-First Access

Patients see exactly what data will be shared and explicitly approve before any doctor can view it. No blanket permissions.

⏱️

Auto-Expiring Sessions

Doctor access sessions expire automatically after 15-30 minutes. No persistent access. Every consultation is a fresh, time-limited window.

🇮🇳

DPDPA Compliant

Built from the ground up for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act. Phone + OTP auth, no unnecessary data collection, full audit trail.

🏥

Verified Doctors Only

Doctors register through hospital-issued invite codes. Every doctor on the platform is tied to a verified hospital or clinic.

📋

Complete Access Log

Patients can see exactly who viewed their data, when, and for how long. Full transparency on every access event.

🔄

Rotating Access Codes

6-digit codes rotate every 60 seconds — like a banking OTP. Even if someone sees a code, it's useless in a minute.
