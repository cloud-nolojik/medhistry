How It Works
Three steps. Thirty seconds. Done.
No training needed. No complex setup. Upload your records once and share them with any doctor, anywhere — using a QR code.
1

You upload your records

Prescriptions, lab reports, discharge summaries — scan paper or upload digital. AI reads and organizes everything into a structured health profile.

2

You show a QR code at the clinic

A rotating QR code appears in your app. You review what will be shared and give consent by showing the QR code to your doctor.

3

Doctor scans, gets a 30-second briefing

Doctor points their camera at your QR code. Instantly sees a structured briefing card — conditions, meds, allergies, labs. Session auto-expires after the visit.

Security & Trust
Built for privacy, designed for India
Your data never lingers. Doctor access is temporary. Everything is consent-based and compliant with Indian data protection law.
🔒

Consent-First Access

You see exactly what data will be shared and explicitly approve before any doctor can view it. No blanket permissions.

⏱️

Auto-Expiring Sessions

Doctor access sessions expire automatically after 15-30 minutes. No persistent access. Every consultation is a fresh, time-limited window.

🇮🇳

DPDPA Compliant

Built from the ground up for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act. QR-based consent, no unnecessary data collection, full audit trail.

🏥

Verified Doctors Only

Doctors register through hospital-issued invite codes. Every doctor on the platform is tied to a verified hospital or clinic.

📋

Complete Access Log

See exactly who viewed your data, when, and for how long. Full transparency on every access event.

🔄

Rotating QR Codes

QR codes rotate every 60 seconds with encrypted tokens. Even if someone photographs a code, it's useless in a minute.
